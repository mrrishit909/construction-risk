Construction Risk

An invented 8-storey tower on day 180 of a 310-day plan. The forecast says when it really finishes, which activities decide that, what a late switchgear delivery and three wet weeks cost, and what a change order does before anyone signs it. …
The project, its 600 activities, the twenty past projects and the site photo are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real building. The photo reader counts coloured panels in a schematic image and says so. No outside model or service is called.
